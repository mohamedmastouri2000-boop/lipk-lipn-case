# LIPK / LIPN case research

Research notes and a candidate therapy design for a reported homozygous deletion of the
epidermal lipase genes **LIPK** and **LIPN** at chromosome band 10q23.31, presenting as
severe autosomal recessive congenital ichthyosis.

**This is a research proposal. It is not medical advice and not a treatment plan.**
Nothing here has been tested in a human. The deletion is taken from a public social media
post and has not been independently verified against a clinical genetics report.

## The short version

Two small secreted enzymes expressed almost only in skin are missing. Without them the
outer skin layers cannot build the lipid water barrier.

Three questions must be answered from the existing sequencing data before anything else:

1. Is the deletion present on both copies of chromosome 10?
2. Does it extend to `LIPA`? Loss causes Wolman disease, which has an approved enzyme
   replacement therapy.
3. Does it extend to `PTEN`? Loss of one copy is a cancer predisposition syndrome needing
   lifelong surveillance.

Five candidate therapies are worked through in the design document. The lead is a topical
gene therapy on a vector platform already approved in humans for a different gene. The
fastest route to benefit is lipidomics of the patient's own skin scale, which also defines
the efficacy biomarker for everything else.

## Status

No institution is working on this. The blocker is not the science. The cheapest next action
is a free submission to Tele-Ichthyosis, a remote consultation platform that puts a case in
front of ichthyosis experts.
